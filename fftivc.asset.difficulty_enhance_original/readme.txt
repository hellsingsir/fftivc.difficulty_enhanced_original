Made for NG/NG+ Playthrough for FFT: The Ivalice Chronicles. Feel overpowered?
Looted every item from the Deep Dungeon in your first playthrough? 
This mod is intended to revamp the NG/NG+ replay value.
Recommended for a challenging playthrough.

Requires FFTIVC Mod Loader & Reloaded II

Installation:
Drag and drop the corresponding .zip file to your Reloaded-II
"Final Fantasy Tactics: The Ivalice Chronicles" "Configure Mods" Section.

Should pop up as "FFTIVC: Difficulty Enhanced - Original Edition"

Ideally put this near the top underneath FFTIVC Mod Loader.
Not sure if this mod conflicts with other mods.
However, there are many changes to Classes, Monsters, Items, Weapons, Armor, Unique Skills, and Story Enemy Encounters.


Updates to V1.01:

Priest -> Replaced Move-HP UP with Halve MP

Features:
-Innate Abilities added to all classes and most special classes.
-Rebalanced Job Skill Growth (Influence from FFT 1.3 Insane Difficulty Mod Job's Skill Growth)
-All Story Battle Enemies have a full set of Secondary, Reactionary, Support, and Movement Abilities. Item Equipment Scales.
-Special Enemy Units added to Story Battles, mostly in Chapter 3 and 4.
-Some Classes have new equippable item slots, ex. Squire has Shield, Monk has Headgear, White Mage has Flail
-New Abilities added to some classes, ex. Squire, Knight, Engineer, Wizard, Priest, Time Mage.
-Reworked abilities, ex: Revive's Vertical changed from 0 to 2.
-Unique Abilities featured.
-Ramza has new ability unlocks at the start of Chapter 2 and Chapter 4.
-Sword Skill Abilities cost MP and no longer have 100% Hit Rate. Follows PHYS Evasion% (Shields, Flanking, Defend, Accessories, Weapon Guard%, Abandon%, Blade Grasp%)
-Rebalances Tactician Mode with the following calculations:
60% DMG Output, 145% DMG Taken, 33% Calc DMG Output, 150% Calc DMG Taken. Enemies have more HP% and Stat Growth, follows Tactician.
-Normal Mode & Easy Mode percentages left at default values. 
-Renamed Most Abilities with Original PSX Ability Names (Some couldn't be renamed due to certain AI's Skill Interactions, ie: Olan & Celestial Stasis).
-Story Battles follow "Harder Story Battles" (Mod created by Zodiark) Level Scaling: Party AVG Level ~+10.
-Hi-Potion, X-Potion, Ethers unlocked at Start of Chapter 2.
-Potions heal 90, Hi-Potions heal 120.
-Ether recovers 30MP, Hi-Ether recovers 60MP.
-Expect to use much more item resources such as Potions, Ethers, Remedies, and Phoenix Downs.
-Thrown Items: Shurikens & Bombs have higher damage.
-Most spells have faster CT.
-Archer's Aim Abilities have much faster CT.
-Evasion increased for most classes and enemies, flanking units is more effective.
-All Shields now have added Magic Evasion % added.
-Armor and Clothes have added HP and MP increases.
-Most Equippable Items now have Added Immunities, Bonuses, Elemental Scaling, or Ability Procs. Check in Each Item's Page 2 Description for Listed Effects.
-Worker 8, Apanda, and all Monsters buffed.
-Monsters have new abilities, innate abilities, and some naturally have Beastmaster. Story Battle Monster Encounters are much more difficult.
-Cloud's Limit Break abilities now have Unit Targeting and buffed CT Speed. Cross-Slash has Disable %. As a trade-off, most Limit Break abilities costs MP.
-Meliadoul as a Guest & Ally now has access to Dark Knight's abilities: Night Sword & Dark Sword.
-New Encounters during Beowulf's Side Quest.
-Reis' Holy Dragon Form is much more powerful.
-Beowulf's Templar Job is now a hybrid of Templar & Onion Knight. He can equip any item/armor, with Onion Knight's Innate Abilities. (Templar Abilities still require a Sword)
-Lucavi Demons+. Expect added abilities, and a much more difficult fight rather than killing each boss before their dialogue ends. Increased HPScaling, HPMultiplier, some have SpeedGrowth and/or SpeedMultiplier Buffs. Added Innate Abilities.
-Mini Bosses Buffed (Miluda, Wiegraf, Gafgarion, Zalmo, Izlude, Vormav, Rofel, Kletian)
-Added Rare Loot Equipment to some enemies throughout Story Encounters and Side Quests.
-Altima is much more powerful in both stages of the final battle, with added abilities and new abilities. Has a lot more HP in both phases, with buffed speed as well. Has Innate Abilities added as well.
-Alma has new added spells and immunities from Altima's transformation. Do your best to keep her alive during the final battle. Replenish her MP to utilize added spells.
